## Trivial-fourth-power commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which the fourth-power map `x ↦ x◇x◇x◇x` is the identity.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→{0,1,2,3}` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map.  The operation is componentwise addition, with `1+3 = 2+2 = 3+1 = 1` and `2+3 = 3+2 = 2` and `3+3 = 3`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
