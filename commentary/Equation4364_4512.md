This conjunction implies that products `x ◇ y` are central (commute with every element).  Any product of four or more variables can be arbitrarily reordered.

The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Σ×Σ×Σ)' ⊔ Map(Σ,ℕ)'` where `(Σ×Σ×Σ)'` is the set of triples `(a,b,c)` quotiented by the equivalence relation `(a,b,c)~(b,c,a)~(c,a,b)`, and `Map(Σ,ℕ)'` is the set of maps whose sum of values is finite and at least 4.
