## Commutative semigroups with a square condition

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which `x ◇ x ◇ y = x ◇ y ◇ y`.  In particular, any power of `x` higher than four is equal to the fourth power.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is a subset `Q(Σ) ⊂ (𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}) × ℕ` consisting of `(A,k)` with `k ≤ max(1, 4 - |A|)`, namely `k ∈ {0,1,2,3}` for `|A|=1`, and `k∈{0,1,2}` for `|A|=2`, and otherwise `k∈{0,1}`, where `|A|` is the cardinal of `A`.  The generators are `({a},0)` for `a∈Σ`.  The operation is `(A, k) ◇ (B, l) = (A ∪ B, m)` with `m = min(k + l + |A ∩ B|, max(1, 4 - |A∪B|))`, namely it is `k + l + |A ∩ B|` where addition saturates at the maximal value allowed in `Q(Σ)`.
