## Commutative semigroups with a square condition

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which `x ◇ x ◇ y = x ◇ y ◇ y`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `(𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}) × ℕ` (where `𝒫` denotes finite subsets) with generators `({a}, 0)` for `a ∈ Σ`, and with the operation `(A, k) ◇ (B, l) = (A ∪ B, k + l + |A ∩ B|)` where `|A|` is the cardinal of `A`.  The map `(A, k) ↦ |A| + k` is a morphism to the additive semigroup `{1,2,3,…}`.
