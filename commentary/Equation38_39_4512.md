## Null semigroups: the constant law

This conjunction of laws is equivalent to the constant [law 46](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?46) `x ◇ y = z ◇ w` which states that the operation takes a constant value.  Such semigroups, called null semigroups, are in one-to-one correspondence with pointed sets.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law consists of `Σ ⊔ {∞}` with `_ ◇ _ = ∞`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
