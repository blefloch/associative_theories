## Trivial-fifth-power semigroups with central squares

This conjunction describes semigroups in which the fifth-power map `x ↦ x◇x◇x◇x◇x` is the identity and squares commute with all elements.

The free semigroup on a single generator for this law has four elements.  For larger sets of generators, the free semigroup is infinite.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
