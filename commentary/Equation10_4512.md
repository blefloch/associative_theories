## Rectangular band law

This law implies idempotence, hence semigroups satisfying `x = x ◇ y ◇ x` are bands.  Moreover, they are rectangular bands, which are direct products of a left-zero band (law 4 `x = x ◇ y`) and a right-zero band (law 5 `x = y ◇ x`).

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ×Σ` (containing `Σ` diagonally), with the operation `(x,y) ◇ (z,w) = (x,w)`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
