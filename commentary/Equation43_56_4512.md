## Abelian groups of exponent 3

This conjunction characterizes commutative groups in which every element cubes to the identity, namely `ℤ/3ℤ`-modules.  Indeed, it implies that left and right multiplications cube to the identity, hence are bijective, so that the semigroup is a group.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the direct sum of `|Σ|` copies of `ℤ/3ℤ`.
