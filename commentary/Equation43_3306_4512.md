## Almost-trivial-cubes commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups that are close to having a trivial cubing map `C:x ↦ x◇x◇x`, in the sense that their set of cubes `B = im(C)` is commutative and has trivial cubes, and that `x◇y = C(x)◇C(y)`.  The cubing map is the identity map on products (namely `C(x◇y)=x◇y`).

Such semigroups are obtained from a commutative semigroup with trivial cubes `B` by duplicating some elements.  In detail, `M = A ⊔ B` with `A ⊂ B` and a cubing map `C : M → B` defined as the inclusion map `A ⊂ B` and the identity on `B`, and the product is defined from the operation on `B` by `x ◇ y = C(x) ◇ C(y)`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Map(Σ,{0,1,2}) ∖ {0})` with (in the notation above) `A = Σ` and `B = Map(Σ,{0,1,2}) ∖ {0}` equipped with the (saturating) component-wise sum operation.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
