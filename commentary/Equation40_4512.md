## The unipotent law

This law states that all squares are equal to the same element `∞`, which is idempotent and central (commutes with every element).  The coset `{∞◇x, x∈M}` is a Boolean group with `∞◇∞ = ∞` as its identity element.

The free semigroup on one generator for this law has three elements.  The free semigroup on two generators `{a, b}` for this law has 10 elements, `{a, b, a◇b, b◇a, a◇b◇a, b◇a◇b, ∞, ∞◇a, ∞◇b, ∞◇a◇b}`.  The free semigroup on a larger alphabet `Σ` is the disjoint union of: the set of square-free words of `Σ`; the finite power set `𝒫(Σ)`.  The operation is concatenation followed by reducing any non-square-free word to an element of the free Boolean group `𝒫(Σ)`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
