## Commutative zero-cubic semigroups

This conjunction implies that all cubic products `x◇y◇z` are equal to the same element `∞` which is a left and right zero, and the operation is commutative.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law consists of multisets with up to 2 elements of `Σ`, with `{a} ◇ {b} = {a,b}` (including for `a=b`) and all other products equal to `∅`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
