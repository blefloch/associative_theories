## Almost-commutative semigroups

This conjunction states that any product of three or more variables can be arbitrarily reordered.

The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ) ⊔ Map(Σ,ℕ)'` where `Map(Σ,ℕ)'` is the set of maps whose sum of values is finite and at least 3.
