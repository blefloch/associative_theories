## Zeropotent zero-cubic semigroups

This conjunction implies that all cubic products `x◇y◇z` and all squares are equal to the same element `∞` which is a left and right zero.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ)' ⊔ {∞}` where `(Σ×Σ)' = {(a, b) | a≠b}`, equipped with the operation `a ◇ b = (a, b)` for `a≠b` and all other products equal to `∞`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
