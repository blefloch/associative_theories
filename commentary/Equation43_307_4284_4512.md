## Commutative semigroups with a square condition

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which `x ◇ x = x ◇ x ◇ x` and `x ◇ x ◇ y = x ◇ y ◇ y`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `(𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}) × {0,1}` (where `𝒫` denotes finite subsets) with generators `({a}, 0)` for `a ∈ Σ`, and with the operation `(A, k) ◇ (B, l) = (A ∪ B, m)` where `m=0` if `k = l = 0` and `A,B` are disjoint, and otherwise `m=1`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
