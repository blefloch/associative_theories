This conjunction implies that squares are central (commute with every element).  Any product of at least four variables, or any product with any duplicated variable, can be arbitrarily reordered.

The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Σ×Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Map(Σ, {0,1,2}) ∖ {0})` modulo an equivalence relation, where `Map(Σ, {0,1,2}) ∖ {0}` can be seen as the collection of non-empty multisets with elements in `Σ` that are at present at most twice.  The equivalence relation identifies `(a,b,c) ~ (c,b,a) ∈ Σ×Σ×Σ` and identifies elements of `(Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Σ×Σ×Σ)` to the corresponding multiset in `Map(Σ, {0,1,2})`.  The operation is `a ◇ b = (a,b)` and `a ◇ (b,c) = (a,b) ◇ c = (a,b,c)` and other operations are given by multiset union, with the number of copies of each element being capped at 2.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
