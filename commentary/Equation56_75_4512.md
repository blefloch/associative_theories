## Groups of exponent 3

This conjunction characterizes groups (not necessarily abelian) in which every element cubes to the identity.  Indeed, it implies that left and right multiplications cube to the identity, hence are bijective, so that the semigroup is a group.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the free group of exponent 3.  In particular, for a finite set of generators it is the Burnside group `B(m,3)` with `m=|Σ|`, and it has `3^{m(m^2+5)/6}` elements.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
