## The Boolean group law

This combination of associativity and the semi-symmetric quasigroup law is equivalent to stating that the semigroup is a Boolean group, namely an abelian group in which all elements square to the identity.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set `𝒫(Σ)` of finite subsets of `Σ` with the symmetric difference operation.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
