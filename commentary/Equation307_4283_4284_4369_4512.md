This conjunction implies that squares are central (commute with every element).  Any product of at least four variables, or any product with any duplicated variable, can be arbitrarily reordered.

The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Σ×Σ×Σ) ⊔ (𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅})` modulo an equivalence relation that identifies `(a,a) ∈ Σ×Σ` and `(a,a,a) ∈ Σ×Σ×Σ` to  `{a} ∈ 𝒫(Σ)`, and `(a,a,b)` and `(a,b,a)` and `(b,a,a)` to `{a,b} ∈ 𝒫(Σ)`, and finally `(a,b,c) ~ (c,b,a) ∈ Σ×Σ×Σ`.  The operation is given by `a ◇ b = (a,b)` and `a ◇ (b,c) = (a,b,c)` and `(a,b) ◇ c = (a,b,c)` and all other products are given by the union of sets in `𝒫(Σ)`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
