## Commutative semigroups

This conjunction of commutativity and associativity describes commutative semigroups.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→ℕ` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map.  Equivalently, it is the direct sum of `|Σ|` copies of `ℕ`, without the origin.  Here `ℕ = {0, 1, …}`.
