## Abelian groups of exponent 4

This conjunction characterizes commutative groups in which the fourth power of every element is the identity, namely `ℤ/4ℤ`-modules.  Indeed, it implies that left and right multiplications to the fourth power are the identity map, hence are bijective, so that the semigroup is a group.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the direct sum of `|Σ|` copies of `ℤ/4ℤ`.
