## A law for sets equipped with an idempotent self-map

This law together with associativity is equivalent to [law 374](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?374) `x ◇ y = (x ◇ x) ◇ x`.  It states that the semigroup operation is left-projection composed with squaring, namely `x◇y = S(x)`, and that `S: x ↦ x◇x` is an idempotent map, namely `S(S(x)) = S(x)`.  Such semigroups are in one-to-one correspondence with sets equipped with an idempotent self-map, or equivalently partitioned into two subsets with a map from one subset to the other.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law consists of `{0,1}×Σ` with `(_,s) ◇ (_,_) = (1,s)`.
