## Commutative semigroups with cubes equal to squares

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups with `x◇x = x◇x◇x`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→{0,1,2}` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map, with the operation given by saturating addition: `0+0=0`, `0+1=1+0=1`, all other additions being equal to `2`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
