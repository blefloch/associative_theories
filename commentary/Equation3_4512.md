## Idempotent semigroups (bands)

Every variety of bands can be defined [by a single identity](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-58.2.323).

This law implies all of the single-variable laws.

The free semigroup with one generator for this law has a single element.  The free semigroup for this law with two generators {a, b} has 6 elements: {a, b, a◇b, b◇a, a◇b◇a, b◇a◇b}.  For larger alphabets the free semigroup is infinite; it consists of square-free words.
