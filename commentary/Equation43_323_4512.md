## Almost-idempotent commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups that are close to being idempotent, in the sense that their set of squares `B = im(S)` (with `S:x ↦ x◇x`) is a commutative band (commutative idempotent semigroup), and that `x◇y = S(x)◇S(y)`.  The squaring map is the identity on products (namely `S(x◇y)=x◇y`).

Such semigroups are obtained from a commutative band `B` by duplicating some elements.  In detail, `M = A ⊔ B` with `A ⊂ B` and a squaring map `S : M → B` defined as the inclusion map `A ⊂ B` and the identity on `B`, and the product is defined from the operation on `B` by `x ◇ y = S(x) ◇ S(y)`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅})` with (in the notation above) `A = Σ` and `B = 𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}` equipped with the union operation.  Here `𝒫(Σ)` is the set of finite subsets of `Σ`.
