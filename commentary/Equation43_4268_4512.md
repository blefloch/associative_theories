## Almost zeropotent commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups such that all `x◇x◇y` are equal to the same element `∞` that is a left and right zero.  Squares need not be all equal.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `(𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}) ⊔ Σ ⊔ {∞}` with the generators `{a}` for `a ∈ Σ`, and with the operation `A ◇ B = A ∪ B` for disjoint subsets, `{a} ◇ {a} = a` for equal singletons, and all other products equal to `∞`.  Here `𝒫(Σ)` is the set of finite subsets of `Σ`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
