This conjunction implies that the set of products `P = {x ◇ y | x,y ∈ M}` is a commutative semigroup.  Any product of four or more variables can be arbitrarily reordered.

The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `Σ ⊔ (Σ×Σ) ⊔ (Σ×Σ×Σ)' ⊔ Map(Σ,ℕ)'` where `(Σ×Σ×Σ)'` is the set of triples `(a,b,c)` quotiented by the equivalence relation `(a,b,c)~(c,b,a)`, and `Map(Σ,ℕ)'` is the set of maps whose sum of values is finite and at least 4.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
