## Trivial-fifth-power commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which the fifth-power map `x ↦ x◇x◇x◇x◇x` is the identity.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→{0,1,2,3,4}` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map.  The operation is componentwise addition, with 4 subtracted from results strictly larger than 4.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
