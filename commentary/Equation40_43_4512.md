## Unipotent commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which all squares are equal to the same element `∞`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is `({0,1}×𝒫(Σ)) ∖ {(0,∅)}` containing `Σ` through the injection `a ↦ (0, {a})`, and with the operation `(s,A) ◇ (t,B) = (u, A Δ B)`, where `u` vanishes if and only if `s = t = 0` and `A ∩ B = ∅`.  Here `𝒫(Σ)` consists of the finite subsets of `Σ` and `Δ` is the symmetric difference operation.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
