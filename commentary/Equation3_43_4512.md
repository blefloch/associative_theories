## Semi-lattices

This conjunction describes commutative bands, namely commutative idempotent semigroups, also called semi-lattices.  Indeed, such semigroups are equipped with a partial order: `x ≤ y` if `x ◇ y = x` for which `◇` is the meet operation.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set `𝒫(Σ) ∖ ∅` of non-empty finite subsets of `Σ`, equipped with the union operation.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
