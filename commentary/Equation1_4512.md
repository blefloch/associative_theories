## Semigroups

The law 1 `x = x` is trivial, so this conjunction reduces to only associativity `x ◇ (y ◇ z) = (x ◇ y) ◇ z`.

A law is implied by the associative law if and only if the left and right hand sides agree after all parentheses have been deleted.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators is the set of finite non-empty lists of elements of `Σ`, equipped with the concatenation operation.
