## Commutative semigroups with idempotent squares

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which the squaring map `S:x ↦ x◇x` is idempotent (`S(S(x)) = S(x)`), or equivalently squares are idempotent elements (`S(x)◇S(x) = S(x)`).

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→{0,1,2,3}` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map, equipped with componentwise addition, with 2 (repeatedly) subtracted from results strictly above 3.
