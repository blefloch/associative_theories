## Zeropotent commutative semigroups with some trivial products

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which all squares, and all products of products, are equal to the same element `∞`, which is a left and right zero.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of subsets of `Σ` with up to 3 elements, with `{a}◇{b} = {a,b}` and `{a}◇{b,c} = {a,b}◇{c} = {a,b,c}` when these elements are pairwise distinct, and all other products equal to `∅`.
