## Zero-cube commutative semigroups with a square condition

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which all cubes are equal to the same element `∞` which is a left and right zero, the product of two squares is also `∞`, and additionally `x ◇ x ◇ y = x ◇ y ◇ y`.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the union of `{∞}` and of the set of maps `(Σ ⊔ {0}) → {0,1}` whose sum of values is finite, excluding the zero map.  One has `∞ ◇ _ = _ ◇ ∞ = ∞`.  To define `f ◇ g` for maps `f,g`, one computes the sum of `f(0) + g(0)` plus the number of `a` such that `f(a) = g(a) = 1`: if this is `≥ 2` then `f ◇ g = ∞`; otherwise `f ◇ g` is a map with `(f ◇ g)(0)` given by this sum and with `(f ◇ g)(a) = max(f(a), g(a))`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
