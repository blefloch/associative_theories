## Trivial-cube commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which the cubing map `x ↦ x◇x◇x` is the identity.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set of maps `Σ→{0,1,2}` whose sum of values is finite, except the zero map.  The operation is componentwise addition, with `1+2 = 2+1 = 1` and `2+2 = 2`.
