## Zeropotent commutative semigroups

This conjunction describes commutative semigroups in which all squares are equal to the same element `∞` that is a (left and right) zero.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law is the set `𝒫(Σ)` of finite subsets of `Σ`, with `A ◇ B` given by `∅` if either set is empty or the two sets intersect, and the union `A ∪ B` if the two sets are non-empty and disjoint.
