## Commutative zeropotent zero-cubic semigroups

This conjunction is very restrictive: all cubic products `x◇y◇z` and all squares are equal to the same element `∞` which is a left and right zero, and the operation is commutative.

The free semigroup on some set `Σ` of generators for this law consists of subsets of `Σ` with up to 2 elements, with `{a} ◇ {b} = {a,b}` for `a≠b` and all other products equal to `∅`.

This conjunction is equivalent to its dual.
