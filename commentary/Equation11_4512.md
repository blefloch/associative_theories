## The right-unit squares law

This law states that squares are right units, hence form a submagma satisfying the left-zero law.

The free semigroup with one generator for this law has two elements.  The free semigroup for this law with two generators {a, b} has 14 elements: {a, b, a◇a, a◇b, b◇a, b◇b, a◇a◇b, a◇b◇a, b◇a◇b, b◇b◇a, a◇a◇b◇a, a◇b◇a◇b, b◇a◇b◇a, b◇b◇a◇b}.  For larger alphabets the free semigroup is infinite.
