The free semigroup on some finite set `Σ` of generators for this law is `A ⊔ (𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅})` where `A` is the set of non-empty ordered subsets of `Σ` (namely non-empty words on the alphabet `Σ` with no repeating letter) and `𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}` the finite non-empty subsets.  The generators are single-letter words in `A`.  The operation is defined on `A` as word concatenation when words have no common letters, while words with repeated letters are replaced by the underlying (unordered) set of letters in `𝒫(Σ) ∖ {∅}`, equipped with the union operation.
